4. GM: Settings, new "Players' lights" card (Torchlight's permissions, under Darkness).

PLAYERS' LIGHTS
Show lights to playersPlayers see the strip, the badges and DIS tags. Off: only their own lights.
Players light their ownTorch, Lantern or Light spell on their own token, one lit at a time.
Players remove their ownOff: they can put lights out and back on, but only you remove them.

Which token is Greg's? A player's lights go on the token they put on the map (createdUserId, the same rule as "Your turn"). A player with no token of their own gets a timer with no map light, like a campfire.

Not for players: Time passes, Pause, Put lights out, the p. 84 settings, carrier and note, Shutter and Refill oil, the Light Tracker view. Shutter and Refill could be added for players later if wanted.

Undo: players' actions aren't on the GM's undo (their lights are in their own data). A player's mistake is fixed with Put out / Resume.
