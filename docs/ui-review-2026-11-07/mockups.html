Q6 · Round 2Death prompt, ordered by how often it happens

Rules (p. 89): each turn the dying character rolls a d20; a natural 20 rises with 1 HP, so 19 in 20 rolls fail. An ally's DC 15 INT check stabilizes; healing ends it. So: Failed is the big button, Stabilized and Healed next, Nat 20 after, Dead tucked away. Today ▶ keeps reopening the prompt until something is picked, which is the freeze. Skip for now moves the turn on with no decision.
